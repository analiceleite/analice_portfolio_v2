A private production app for worship ministries with 30+ users. It manages songs, repertoires, bimonthly schedules, role-based access for admins/ministers/media, email reminders, YouTube search, CifraClub/Cifralize integrations, and PWA support. Public demo is unavailable because the system serves a closed church environment.
