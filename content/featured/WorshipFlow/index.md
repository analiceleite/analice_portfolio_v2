A multi-tenant production app for worship ministries with 30+ users. It manages songs, repertoires, bimonthly schedules, role-based access for admins/ministers/media, email reminders, YouTube search, CifraClub/Cifralize integrations, and PWA support. Recruiters and guests can test the isolated demo tenant with `demo@worshipflow.app` / `Demo@12345`.
