A SaaS-style management platform for thrift stores. It centralizes inventory, customers, suppliers, sales, live shopping, shopping bags, shipments, notes, financial tracking, tenant administration, authentication, and PWA support. Demo tenant available: `demo@brechoteste.com` / `crissia-demo123`.
